✏️ ব্যাকরণ টিপ

have / has — “আছে”

  • I / You / We / They have
  • He / She / It has — তৃতীয় ব্যক্তিতে has
  • না বোঝাতে: don't have (সে হলে doesn't have)
শুরুবসবেউদাহরণ
IhaveI have a brother.
ShehasShe has a sister.
TheyhaveThey have a car.
🧠 নিজে চেষ্টা করুন: My sister ___ a pen.
have / has / having

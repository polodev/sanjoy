✏️ ব্যাকরণ টিপ

There is / There are — “আছে”

  • There is + একটি জিনিস: There is a fan.
  • There are + একাধিক: There are two rooms.
  • কোথায়: on (উপর), under (নিচে), in (ভিতরে), next to (পাশে)
শুরুবসবেউদাহরণ
There isএকটিThere is a bed.
There areএকাধিকThere are three chairs.
🧠 নিজে চেষ্টা করুন: There ___ three chairs in the room.
is / are / am

উত্তর: are — three chairs একাধিক, তাই There are।
